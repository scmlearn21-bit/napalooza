## Project Overview
Napalooza is a wearable headband designed to acheive the goal of getting the most out of sleep.
It utilizes different technology to track sleep cycles to create a personalized experience for each user.

## The Problem
Sleep quality affects many aspects of well-being but many people struggle to fit sleep into their modern day busy lifestyle.

## The Solution
Our headband designed to help the user both fall asleep better and maximize their sleep time for healthier sleep.
We use vibration, sound, and various sensors such as EEG sensors to achieve this goal.
